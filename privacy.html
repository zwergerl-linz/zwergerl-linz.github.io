Privacy

Zwergerl is a game about places in Linz, and it is in a field test. This page says what it stores about you, why, for how long, and who can see it. You agree to it before you play. If it changes in a way that matters, it gets a new version and you are asked again.

Some of this is not switched on yet. For now, claims and their photos are not saved, spots you add stay on your phone, and no analytics are sent. Those parts say planned.

Location

Zwergerl keeps no location trail. Your position is used while the app is open, to show distances and to check that you are at a spot. It is not stored. Two things that are stored do say where you were:

  • A claim says that you were at that spot at that time. It holds your distance from the spot, not your coordinates.
  • A spot that you add has its position, which is where you stood.

Your phone asks you before Zwergerl can use your location. Without it you can’t claim spots. The compass and the camera are used for claims and for adding a spot, and nothing else.

What is stored, and why

Account
Your email address, a hash of your password, and when you registered, so that you can sign in and your progress stays yours. Each time you sign in, the time, your IP address and what your browser says it is are stored too, which is how the sign-in service works. The same goes for asking for an email to reset your password or confirm your address, and for opening its link. The account exists from when you register, so before you agree to this page.
Display name
So that other players recognise you on the map and the leaderboard. Every player who has agreed can see it, and anyone can find out whether a name is taken. The account and the name are the same in the Zwergerl app.
Your agreement
Which version of this page you agreed to last, and when.
Claims (planned)
The spot, the time, the points, whether you claimed, reclaimed or stole it, whether demo controls were on, and four readings: your distance from the spot, your GPS accuracy, how far off your phone pointed, and how long you stayed. They decide who holds a spot and the points, and show the team which checks fail. Other players see which spots you hold and since when, and your points and number of claims on the leaderboard, and whoever you take a spot from is told. They can’t see your readings.
Proof photos (planned)
The photo you take for a claim, as proof that you were there. Other players can’t see it. It is never compared with another photo automatically, and it is stored without location data inside the file.
Spots you add (planned)
The name, the position, the heading you faced and the reference photo, so that others can find and claim the spot. Every player can see them.
Test analytics (planned)
That you signed up or signed in, opened a spot or the leaderboard, started a claim and how far it got, which check stopped it, that you added a spot, and how long you had the app open. Linked to your account. They show the team how many people take part and where they get stuck. No coordinates, no email address and nothing that you type.

On this device

Your browser keeps your session, so that you stay signed in, and for now also the spots you added, with their photos. Signing out removes all of it from the device. There is no advertising and no tracking.

Who can see it

  • Other players: what is said above, and nothing else.
  • The people who run the test: everything in the database, if they need to. They can’t see your password.

For how long

Zwergerl doesn’t delete anything automatically yet. A spot becomes free again after 14 days without a reclaim, but the claim stays stored.

Deleting your data

A button in the app that deletes your account is planned. It will remove your account and display name, the spots you hold, your claims, your analytics and your photos. Because the account is shared, it will also end your login in the Zwergerl app, where spots that you hold stay under your old name.
